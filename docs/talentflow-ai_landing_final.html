How it works

From resume to the right job in four steps

  1. 01

    Save your resume

    Paste text or upload a .txt or text-based PDF. You keep one resume on your account.

  2. 02

    We read your skills

    Skills are picked out of your resume and out of each job listing.

  3. 03

    Compare by meaning

    Your resume and each listing are compared by skills and by meaning, not only exact words.

  4. 04

    See your score, then apply

    Every listing shows your score and the skills you lack. Apply where you fit.

Questions

Things people ask

Short answers about the score, your resume and who sees what.

Is the score a hiring decision?

No. It is a ranking that shows how closely a resume matches a listing. Recruiters make every decision, and a low score never blocks an application.

What resume formats work?

Pasted text, a .txt file, or a text-based PDF. Scanned or image PDFs cannot be read. For a PDF, you review the extracted text before saving it.

Can a recruiter see my resume before I apply?

No. A recruiter sees your resume only after you apply to one of their listings.

Why is a skill marked as missing?

The listing asks for it and your saved resume does not mention it. If you have the skill, add it to your resume and your score updates.
